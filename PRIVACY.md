# 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

适用于：扫弦教练 / StrumCoach（iOS 和 Android）
生效日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日

[简体中文](#简体中文) · [English](#english)

## 简体中文

**简单说：这个 App 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何数据。**

### 我们不收集任何数据

- App 没有账号，不需要登录或注册。
- App 不联网：iOS 版没有任何网络代码，Android 版没有申请联网权限。
- 没有广告、没有统计分析、没有崩溃上报，也没有接入任何广告、统计或追踪类的第三方 SDK（只用了苹果和谷歌的系统库）。
- 没有内购，不处理任何付款信息。

### 麦克风

麦克风只用于调音器、给扫弦练习打分，以及在 iOS 版里录制你自己的节拍音色。声音在手机上实时分析，分析完就丢弃，不保存录音（自己录的节拍音色除外，它只存在你的手机里），也不会发送到任何地方。你可以随时在系统设置里关闭麦克风权限，关闭后调音和扫弦打分不能用，其他功能不受影响。

### 存在手机上的数据

练习记录、设置、收藏的和弦、自定义的节奏型和和弦进行、自己录的节拍音色，都只保存在你的手机里，我们看不到。删除 App 就会一起删除。

### 儿童

App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

### 开源

App 的全部源代码公开在 <https://github.com/aquila0625/StrumCoach>，任何人都可以查看上面这些说法。

### 变更和联系方式

如果以后政策有变化，会更新这个页面并修改生效日期。有问题请在 <https://github.com/aquila0625/StrumCoach/issues> 提出。

## English

**In short: this app does not collect, upload or share any of your data.**

### No data collection

- There are no accounts; you never sign in or sign up.
- The app does not use the network: the iOS app contains no networking code, and the Android app does not request the internet permission.
- There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or tracking (only the platform libraries from Apple and Google).
- There are no in-app purchases, and no payment information is handled.

### Microphone

The microphone is used only for the tuner, for scoring your strumming practice and, on iOS, for recording your own metronome sound. Audio is analyzed live on the device and discarded; recordings are not kept (except a metronome sound you record yourself, which stays on your phone) and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn off microphone access in the system settings at any time; tuning and strum scoring then stop working and everything else keeps working.

### Data stored on your device

Practice records, settings, favourite chords, custom strumming patterns and chord progressions, and a metronome sound you record yourself are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

### Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

### Open source

The full source code is public at <https://github.com/aquila0625/StrumCoach>, so anyone can check these statements.

### Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date. For questions, open an issue at <https://github.com/aquila0625/StrumCoach/issues>.
